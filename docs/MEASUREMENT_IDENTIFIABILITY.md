# Measurement and identifiability v1

Synthetic fixtures establish expected software outputs for declared inputs and
clock assumptions. Snapshot and duration estimands depend on the observed
sampling path. Hidden ticks, actual broker independence, clock accuracy and
completeness of real feeds remain unknown. Duration and recovery may be
censored. Quoted prices do not identify fill probability, realized costs,
broker intent, dealer inventory, routing or executable arbitrage.

Record declared inputs as FACT, computed quoted values as MEASUREMENT,
interpretations as INFERENCE, testable proposals as HYPOTHESIS, unresolved
quantities as UNKNOWN and discarded claims as REJECTED. A synthetic replay
makes no statistical or causal inference about real markets.
